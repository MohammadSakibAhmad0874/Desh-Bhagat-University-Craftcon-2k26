TRANSMISSION ARCHIVE

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about registering, arriving, hacking, and claiming bounties at CRAFTCON '26.

💬 [LIBRARIAN VILLAGER] Who is eligible to participate in CRAFTCON '26?

Any currently enrolled student (Undergraduate, Postgraduate, or Polytechnic/Diploma) from any recognized academic institution across India is eligible. You will be required to present your official college ID during on-campus check-in.

🪙 [TRADE MASTER] What is the registration fee? Are meals and lodging included?

Affordable Entry Fee: Hackathon registration is ₹300 per builder (1 Builder = ₹300, 2 Builders = ₹600, 3 Builders = ₹900, 4 Builders = ₹1,200). All accepted builders are provided with free 24-hour campus workspace, round-the-clock meals, midnight snacks, unlimited beverages, high-speed Wi-Fi, and designated sleeping/rest quarters on the Desh Bhagat University campus.

🛡️ [GUILD CAPTAIN] What is the allowed team size? Can I apply alone?

Teams can range from 1 to 4 crafters. You can register with an already formed squad, or join our official Discord server to find teammates during the pre-event mixer.

⚙️ [REDSTONE GOLEM] Can we work on an existing project before the hackathon?

No. To maintain complete fairness, all code, designs, and architectural components must be created from scratch after the opening bell at 12:00 PM on October 24. Utilizing publicly available libraries, APIs, and boilerplate templates is completely permitted.

🗺️ [CARTOGRAPHER] How do we reach Desh Bhagat University?

Desh Bhagat University is located at Mandi Gobindgarh, Punjab (along National Highway 44). It is well connected by rail (Sirhind Junction / Mandi Gobindgarh station) and road from Chandigarh, Ludhiana, and Delhi. Shuttle buses will be arranged from key transit hubs for verified outstation participants.

🔬 [ALCHEMIST] Will hardware components or microcontrollers be provided?

For teams participating in Track 03: Redstone Lab, basic sensor modules, ESP32 boards, soldering stations, and 3D printing assistance will be available in the Maker Lab. Teams with specialized hardware needs are welcome to bring their own kits.
