VILLAGER HELPDESK

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

What is the registration fee?

The registration fee is strictly ₹50 per person. For a 4-player squad (BGMI / Free Fire), total is ₹200. For 5-player Mobile Legends, total is ₹250. For Solo games (Ludo, Chess, Carrom), total is ₹50.

How does squad vs solo registration work?

When you click "REGISTER NOW", selecting an Online Game automatically loads the Squad registration form (collecting Team Name, Captain, and Player details). Selecting an Offline Game automatically loads the Solo participant form.

Can I register for multiple games?

Yes! You can complete separate registrations for different games provided there are no schedule conflicts during the tournament days.

How is payment verified?

Payment is processed through our server-side verified gateway (Razorpay / Sandbox UPI). Once completed, the SQLite database stores your confirmed status and generates your unique Registration ID and official downloadable receipt.
